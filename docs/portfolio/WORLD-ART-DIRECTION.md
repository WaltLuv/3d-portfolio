# World Art Direction

The world should feel like the cinematic portfolio version of the 3D PropControl Empire game.

## Physical world

Use:
- residential properties
- multifamily buildings
- lots
- roads
- sidewalks
- driveways
- landscaping
- property signage
- maintenance activity
- inspections
- vendors
- projects / construction
- practical street/property lighting

Materials should feel warm, tactile, architectural, and believable:
- wood
- brick
- glass
- concrete
- metal
- asphalt
- grass
- siding / roofing

## AI layer

AI intelligence should feel distinct:
- holographic cyan
- electric blue
- controlled violet
- scan planes
- data paths
- computer-vision markers
- task movement
- emissive intelligence
- dimensional annotations

Do not make the whole world cyan.

The visual metaphor is:

REAL WORLD
ILLUMINATED BY
AI INTELLIGENCE

## Lighting

Physical:
- warm windows
- practical lights
- soft night ambience
- readable silhouettes
- architectural shadows

AI:
- selective cyan emission
- rim light
- scan illumination
- controlled bloom

Avoid flooding the scene with glow.

## Materials

Audit and improve:
- plastic buildings
- unrealistic gloss
- flat dark surfaces
- poor glass
- uniform material response
- excessive emissive effects

## Environmental life

Use subtle, performant ambient behaviors:
- windows turning on
- streetlights
- fog
- restrained foliage motion
- distant traffic
- maintenance activity
- small property events
- AI pulses
- inspection markers
- workforce task motion
