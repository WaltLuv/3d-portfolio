# Experience Vision

## North Star

Transform the site from "a good portfolio with 3D" into:

> an immersive interactive real-estate AI world that happens to be Walter Thornton's professional portfolio.

It should feel:
- cinematic
- alive
- game-like
- explorable
- story-driven
- premium
- technically sophisticated
- professionally credible

Experience-quality references include the principles behind Igloo, Bruno Simon, Maxime Guillon, and Basement Studio. Do not copy their graphics. Match their level of imagination, movement, worldbuilding, camera choreography, polish, and discovery.

## Four jobs of the site

The final product must simultaneously function as:

1. cinematic 3D experience
2. game-like interactive real-estate world
3. professional employer-facing portfolio
4. product ecosystem hub

## Persistent world

Preserve and improve the persistent-world architecture already merged.

Preferred flow:

Arrival
→ Walter Intro
→ Real Estate World
→ VoiceOps Event
→ PropControl Operation
→ VisionOps Inspection
→ Repair Cost Guide
→ Workforce OS
→ Baseline Studios / Arkitech
→ Builder Studio
→ Built From Operations
→ Contact
→ Final World Reveal

Avoid reverting to disconnected canvases or stacked 3D demos.

## Game feel

Primary navigation: scroll.

Secondary interaction may include:
- pointer parallax
- hover reactions
- click/tap
- drag/orbit in designated areas
- inspection markers
- property interactions
- optional WASD only where appropriate

WASD must never be required. A recruiter must be able to understand the whole story by scrolling.

## Camera

Treat camera work like filmmaking:
- establishing shots
- dolly moves
- tracking
- flythrough
- close inspection
- orbit reveals
- pullbacks
- pauses and stillness
- final scale reveal

Every major view needs intentional foreground, midground, background, focal subject, and negative space for text.

## Mobile

Mobile is the mobile edit of the same film, not a different website.

Preserve world identity and major product moments while reducing geometry, effects, shadows, particles, and secondary motion.

## Reduced motion

Replace elaborate movement with shorter transitions, fades, state changes, and reduced parallax. Do not disable the experience entirely.
