# VisionOps + Repair Cost Guide — The World Can See

VisionOps is one of the signature chapters of the entire portfolio.

## Core narrative

Capture
→ Analyze
→ Findings
→ Repair Cost
→ Action

The visitor should watch VisionOps act on a physical property rather than reading a feature description.

## Recommended sequence

1. Camera approaches the affected property/unit.
2. Inspection mode activates.
3. A cyan scan plane traverses the structure or affected room.
4. Architectural edges/components receive restrained computer-vision treatment.
5. One or more illustrative findings become visible.
6. Camera focuses on the affected component.
7. The finding becomes structured repair intelligence.
8. Repair Cost Guide activates.
9. An illustrative repair estimate/quote assembles visibly.
10. The resulting artifact flows into the operational ecosystem.

Possible illustrative findings include drywall moisture, ceiling damage, flooring exposure, or another scenario that fits the existing product story. Do not imply real customer data if none is available.

## Repair Cost Guide — non-negotiable signature moment

Repair Cost Guide quote/estimate generation must not be buried in copy or represented only by a text card.

The visual logic should make the transformation understandable:

Physical evidence
→ identified component
→ repair scope
→ repair cost intelligence
→ estimate / quote artifact
→ operational action

The quote/estimate can become a dimensional artifact that travels into Workforce OS and/or PropControl.

## Visual principles

Use AI overlays selectively:
- scan plane
- component outline
- finding marker
- measurement / scope cues
- dimensional repair annotations
- estimate assembly

Avoid covering the entire house in permanent cyan wireframe.

## Product meaning

VisionOps represents:

THE WORLD CAN SEE.

Repair Cost Guide represents the bridge from visual evidence to economic/operational action.

## Success test

A visitor should be able to explain, without reading a long paragraph, that VisionOps captures property evidence, analyzes it, identifies findings, creates repair-cost intelligence, and sends the result into an operational workflow.
